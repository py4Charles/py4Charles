### ⭐ Adwuma

**Adwuma** — *Artisan recruitment page*

An end-to-end system that helps individuals in search of everyday services(cleaning, carpentery) to find the right people, for the right price without any hassle. No install required.

- 🎯 **Verified Workforce** : Trusted and verified artisans who know have immense amount of experience in the field.
- 📐 **Dual sign-up initiative** : Both customer and artisans are eligible to sign and join in the platform both as a business seeking to gain
market value or a customer ready to purchase a service.
- 🎨 **Full account customization** : Full customisable features for individuals and businesses to make personalized changes to their accounts.
